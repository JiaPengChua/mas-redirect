Booking redirect — demo

This page stands in for the MAS website. It hosts the malaysiaairlines-sandbox chat with the booking redirect handler. When the bot hands off a booking, this page opens the booking page and reports the outcome to Ada.

  1. Open the chat and run test flight booking (sign in for express booking, or continue as a guest).
  2. After you choose a cabin, the bot says it's taking you to the booking page and this tab moves there.
  3. In the Ada dashboard, the conversation's variables show booking_handler and booking_redirect_status (submitted or failed, with a reason).
